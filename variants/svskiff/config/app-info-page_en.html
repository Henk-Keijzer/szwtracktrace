Usage
With this app you can replay the course of a number of rowing competitions (accelerated) and, if the race is still in progress, follow it live. Click on the name of the race at the top of the screen and choose a race, the year in which it was held and, if necessary, the day or the heat. The race will then be loaded. When the race is loaded, you use the start/stop button at the bottom left of the screen to set the boats in motion. You determine the playback speed with the vertical slider.

Met de "kaart"-knop kunt u uit verschillende kaartachtergronden kiezen.

With the participants menu you can determine how the app automatically follows some, or all, boats. With the checkmarks behind the boats you keep the checked boats in the middle of the screen. With the "All boats on/off" checkmark you can turn all the checkmarks behind the boats on and off at once. With "zoom" at the bottom right of the screen you ensure that the checked boats do not disappear from view.

In a "live" race, you can display the earlier course of the race accelerated by sliding the time slider at the bottom of the screen to the left. Once at the end, it automatically switches back to live.

If you see a small star behind the name of a boat ('Doordrijver'), this means that the received GPS position is not current. The position indicated on the map is then not necessarily the actual position.

The tracks are stored locally on your phone/tablet/laptop/PC as much as possible. However, information must be downloaded from the server regularly. If you are short on your data plan, only choose an earlier race if you are on Wifi.
