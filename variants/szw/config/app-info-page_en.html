Usage
With this app, you can replay the course of several sailing races (fast-forwarded) and, if the race is still ongoing, follow it live. Click on the name of the race at the top of the screen and choose a race, the year it was sailed, and, if necessary, the day. The race will then be loaded. Once the race is loaded, use the start/stop button at the bottom left of the screen to start the vessels moving. Use the vertical slider to set the replay speed.

In the top right of the screen, you will see the buttons for the Participants Menu and the Map Menu.

With the Participants Menu, you can specify how the app automatically follows some or all of the vessels. With the "All vessels on/off" checkbox, you can turn all the checkboxes next to the vessels on and off at once.
A double-click on a vessel's icon on the map selects only that vessel to follow. Simultaneously, the participants menu opens, and you can check one or more additional vessels to follow.
The "follow" and "zoom" buttons at the bottom right of the screen ensure that all checked vessels do not disappear from view.
If you click on the name of a vessel in the Participants Menu, you will see additional information about that vessel (if available).

With the Map Menu, you can choose from different map backgrounds and display or hide other detailed information on the map.

For a "live" race, you can display the earlier course of the race accelerated by sliding the time slider at the bottom of the screen to the left and starting the replay. Once at the end, it automatically switches back to live.

If you see a small asterix after the name of a vessel (e.g., Name'), it means that the received GPS position is not current. The position shown on the map is not necessarily the actual position.

The tracks are stored locally on your phone/tablet/laptop/PC as much as possible. However, information must be regularly downloaded from the server. If your data plan is limited, choose an earlier race only when you are on Wi-Fi.

Maps
The standard map, the gray map, and the map with blue water accents are from the Kadaster. The satellite images are from ©Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community. The rights of the Open Street Map and Open Sea Map belong to ©Open Street Map creators and ©Open Sea Map creators. The overlay map with waterway markings is from Rijkswaterstaat. Unfortunately, not all buoys on the inland waterways are included.

Windy.com
Before or during a live race, you will see a button for Windy in the top-right menu. If you click on it, a window with the wind forecast from Windy.com will open.

Buienradar
Buienradar provides the wind data during the events. On average, every half hour, more than 50 weather stations transmit their current data to www.buienradar.nl. And we are allowed to use it. If wind information is available, you can turn the wind arrows and the wind simulation on and off on the map in the Maps Menu.
Note: the wind simulation costs quite a bit of processing power. If you watch mobile for a long time, your battery can drain quickly.

AIShub
During events such as the Beurtveer, the Strontrace (heavy class), the Slag in de Rondte, and the Waddenrace, the services of AIShub.net are used to track the vessels.
